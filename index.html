    Helsinki City Bikes

    Live availability for every HSL city bike station in Helsinki and Espoo, refreshed every minute from the official GBFS feed.

    • Pin number — bikes available right now.
    • Pin colour — green: plenty, amber: a few, grey: none.
    • Search — station names, plus streets, addresses and places (e.g. "EMMA") via Photon / OpenStreetMap.
    • Nearest bike — walking route from you to the closest station with a bike, via Valhalla / OpenStreetMap.
